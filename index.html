Add a YouTube channel

Add a channel by copying its YouTube URL. You can also use its @handle.

  1. On YouTube, open the channel you want and copy its URL from your browser's address bar.
  2. In Edenia, open Add above your video list and paste the URL.
  3. Click Add to add the channel to your study feed.

What is Anki?

Anki is a flashcard app that schedules reviews to help you remember words and ideas over time. Using Anki with Edenia is optional.

Connect to Anki

Edenia can count your Anki reviews automatically. To let Edenia talk to Anki, install AnkiConnect and allow Edenia in its settings.

  1. Open Anki. In Tools, click Add-ons, then Get Add-ons, then paste this code: 2055492159.
  2. In Add-ons, click AnkiConnect, then Config. Make sure the text after "...," is at the end of the config.
    { ..., "webCorsOriginList": [ "https://bricechivu.github.io" ] }
  3. Restart Anki and keep Anki open while you use Edenia.

How points work

Points reward video study time and Anki reviews. Edenia adds each source before rounding down the total points of the day.

  • Watching 1 hour of video gives 3 pts.
  • 60 Anki reviews gives 2 pts.

Typical Edenia workflow

  • Watch videos from the channels you've added.
  • Use Add to paste either a YouTube video or channel URL.
  • Check your studies with the study history summary and heatmap.
  • Watch your town grow.
