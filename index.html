What is Anki?

Anki is a flashcard app that schedules reviews to help you remember words and ideas over time. Using Anki with Edenia is optional.

Connect to Anki

Edenia can count your Anki reviews automatically. To let Edenia talk to Anki, install AnkiConnect and allow Edenia in its settings.

  1. Open Anki. In Tools, click Add-ons, then Get Add-ons, then paste this code: 2055492159.
  2. In Add-ons, click AnkiConnect, then Config. Copy:
    ,"https://bricechivu.github.io"
    and paste it inside the webCorsOriginList bracket list. For example, you can paste it after:
    "http://localhost"
    In the end, it should look similar to:
    { "apiKey": null, "apiLogPath": null, "ignoreOriginList": [], "webBindAddress": "127.0.0.1", "webBindPort": 8765, "webCorsOriginList": [ "http://localhost", "https://bricechivu.github.io" ] }
  3. Restart Anki and keep Anki open while you use Edenia.

How points work

Points reward video study time and Anki reviews.

  • Watching 1 hour of video gives 30 pts.
  • 60 Anki reviews gives 20 pts.
