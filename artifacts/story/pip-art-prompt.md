# Pip environment atlas provenance

Asset: `images/pip/world.png`.

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The generated atlas was copied unchanged into the project. Characters, props, idle motion, and action effects are authored in the canvas renderer and layered over the six environments.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE production game background atlas image for an original gentle pixel-art duck adventure. This image will be used directly in a browser game as six separate background scenes. Exact atlas layout: 2 equal columns and 3 equal rows, edge-to-edge rectangular panels, NO gutters, NO outlines separating panels, no labels, no lettering. Entire image landscape 1536 by 1024 if possible, making each panel wide 768 by 341. Crisp true pixel art like a beautifully crafted 16-bit handheld adventure, slightly overhead 3/4 view, visible consistent pixel clusters, limited harmonious palette of teal water, lush jade and sage foliage, golden sunlight, warm umber outlines and creamy highlights. NOT vector, not painted, no smooth gradients. Each panel must have an open central midground around horizontal center and one-third down from its top, to place independently animated animal sprites. Keep important scenery in upper 65 percent, lower 35 percent will be partly covered by a dialogue box. No animals, people, ducks, butterflies, frogs, or characters baked into background. TOP LEFT: tranquil opening pond, winding turquoise water with mossy banks, cattails, reeds, a low overhead branch at upper left, tiny lily pads, warm morning light. TOP RIGHT: garden bank path, oversized flowers and leafy hedges at duck scale, warm earthen path, small acorn shells and mushrooms, stream along one side. MIDDLE LEFT: lush lily pools, large lily pads, violet iris flowers, shady low roots forming a small tunnel, inviting stepping stones, turquoise water. MIDDLE RIGHT: Reedbank, a tiny marsh village made from reeds, hollow stumps and leaves, several little homes with golden windows, wooden footbridges, open central shallow pool for a tadpole nursery. BOTTOM LEFT: river crossing, wider teal river curving around a sand bank, small wooden crossing/signpost without text, reeds and a sheltered edge route, gently stronger current farther back. BOTTOM RIGHT: main river landing, wooden jetty and modest reed-roof shelter, an empty tiny leaf/acorn boat, glowing warm late-afternoon water, tranquil reeds, distant river continuing beyond frame. All six places belong to the same cozy original world with strong professional pixel-art detail and consistent scale. No distant large willow tree, no tall human buildings, no UI, no text, no watermarks. This is one atlas asset, not a mockup.
